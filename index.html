01Approach

Greybox breathes through light, shape and composition.

If a blockout does not read with flat materials, it will not read with final art either.

Light

  • One bright pool says "go here"
  • Key lights placed early
  • Most of the frame stays dark

Shape

  • Silhouette before detail
  • One dominant shape per area
  • Scale contrast gives weight

Composition

  • Doors and windows as frames
  • Railings and grids as leading lines
  • Fog layers the depth

05About

Readable spaces, with a little uncertainty.

Hi, I'm Maciej, a Senior Level Designer with 7+ years of experience in puzzle and gameplay design.

I work primarily in Unreal Engine 5. Over my career, I've created thousands of match-3 levels that experimented with the player's ability to learn through play. In my work, I always aim to catch glimpses of intrigue and uncertainty in the player's decision-making.

My craft is shaped by, and shapes, industry-standard gameplay and design practices. I'm looking to grow as a game developer on projects that help shape the future of games.
