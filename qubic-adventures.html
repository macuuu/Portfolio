Qubic Adventures

A multiplayer puzzle game. Player one must reach the destination. Player two shifts the board in sequence to help or to block the path. Our team made a playable prototype for Nintendo Switch.

01Context

One board, two players

Qubic is built on one simple rule: two players share one board, but they do not have the same goal. Player one moves a character to the destination. Player two moves parts of the board in a fixed sequence. Each move can open the path or close it.

I worked on the concept and the level design: the puzzles and the environmental story.

02Puzzles

Complex puzzles from simple moves

The board is made of cubes. Groups of cubes move together, so one move from player two changes many paths at the same time. A puzzle is good when both players must think about the order of the moves, not only about the next step.

The two roles make each level work in two ways: player two can help player one, or work against them. The same board must be solvable in co-operation and still fair when the players compete.

03Environmental story

A world told by the board

The levels tell a small story without text. Fences, a treasure chest, trees and clouds turn an abstract grid into a place, and they show the player where the goal is.
