What I bring

Content that holds players and supports the business

In a live-service game, each level is part of the economy, the session and the retention curve. I design with these goals in mind.

01

Difficulty and pacing

Difficulty curves for the full session and the full content cycle, not for one level. Easy wins, tension peaks and recovery in the correct order.

02

LiveOps content

A steady flow of new levels and special content for live events. Content pacing and level structures that fit the LiveOps economy.

03

Data-driven iteration

I change levels from retention and funnel data. Small changes, tested often. I trust the data more than my own taste.

04

Onboarding

New mechanics that the player learns through play. Each new rule comes on a clear board first, then in combination with others.

05

Premium content

I owned Special Level Content for the highest-value product. Content for engaged, high-value players needs extra care and polish.

06

Team and standards

Review standards, peer reviews and mentoring for junior designers. Close work with product, art, engineering and analytics.
