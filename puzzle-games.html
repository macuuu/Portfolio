01Traffic Puzzle

My first professional project

Traffic Puzzle is an innovative match-3 game with over 10 million players. It was my first professional project. I made levels for it from the prototype stage, and I tried to make them viral and fun.

The project had a big role in my growth as a designer, on many stages of game development and product maintenance.

02Best Fiends

Design in an established live service

Best Fiends is a mobile match-3 puzzle adventure with unique gameplay mechanics. Since its launch in 2014, it has over 100 million downloads.

For me, it was a major design journey in a well-established project. I was responsible for content design and for the development of the level editor. It was also a new chapter in my life: an office in a different city, and the chance to work with industry experts.

03What I learned

Imbalance draws attention

After years of puzzle design, I found that imbalance draws attention. A board that is a little unfair, a little too full or a little asymmetric makes the player look closer.

I take three habits from mobile puzzles into 3D level design: iterate fast, trust the data more than my taste, and plan the difficulty curve for the whole session, not only for one level.
